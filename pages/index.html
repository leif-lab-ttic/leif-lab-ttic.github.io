Research themes

Questions we are investigating

Transformer expressivity

Characterizing what problems neural networks like transformers can and cannot compute, and how architectural choices shape models' computational power.

Formal language theory

Applying automata, logic, and circuit complexity to understand neural networks and language as well as exploring fundamental questions in these areas.

Science of building LLMs

Using theoretical insights to inform the practice of building LLMs, both at the level of architecture and optimization.
